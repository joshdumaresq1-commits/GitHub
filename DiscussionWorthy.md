## Discussion Worthy — 2026-05-28

### Health

- Track key biomarkers every 6 months: HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipid panel, ApoB — looking lean doesn't mean you're metabolically healthy
- Longevity is about quality of your last 10–20 years, not just total lifespan — optimize for healthspan, not just lifespan
- Wear shoes outside only — never inside the home (reduces toxin/pathogen load)
- Moisturize your face daily starting now — one of the highest ROI anti-aging habits
- Most people are severely fiber-deficient; psyllium husk supplementation is a practical daily fix
- Chronic inflammation shows up as belly fat, joint pain, and skin issues before disease — address it proactively
- Build recovery into your week intentionally — elite performance requires scheduled rest, not just working until you crash
- 4 minutes of vigorous exercise (e.g. sprint intervals) can cut death risk by ~40% — even short bouts matter enormously

### Fitness

- Eat the same few high-protein meals repeatedly — consistency, not variety, is what drives body composition results
- Systems beat motivation for fitness — build a repeatable structure, not goal-based willpower
- 2 hard sets per exercise to failure can be sufficient for muscle building — more volume isn't always better
- If using GLP-1 drugs (Ozempic), pair with resistance training and high protein to protect muscle mass; stopping without building habits leads to rapid fat regain
- Visceral fat requires specific intervention (diet quality, sleep, stress reduction) beyond just general weight loss
- Replace cardio obsession with strength training + walking — sufficient for fat loss without chronic cardio stress

### Nutrition

- Prioritize protein and fiber at every meal — the two most under-consumed macronutrients for health and satiety
- Eliminate or drastically reduce: refined white carbs, ultra-processed foods, seed oils, alcohol, and added sugar — especially after 40
- Use a non-sugar sweetener (e.g. allulose, monk fruit) instead of sugar as a default swap
- A greens powder or morning drink (e.g. AG1 equivalent) can serve as daily nutrient insurance if diet is inconsistent
- Eat local and seasonal produce when possible — nutritional quality and environmental impact are both better
- Don't rely on appearance alone to assess diet quality — get bloodwork to confirm metabolic health

### Productivity

- Plan tomorrow the night before with a 15-minute shutdown ritual — opening your laptop without priorities means the day runs you
- Reactive work (email, Slack) costs roughly 13 weeks per year in lost deep work time — protect mornings
- Build systems, not goals — goals get abandoned after achievement; systems sustain behavior change

### Mindset

- Use AI to think harder, not less — the best prompts create psychological pressure and surface blindspots, not just convenience
- Ask AI: 'What are my biggest blindspots based on everything I've shared?' — forces uncomfortable self-reflection
- AI is a sycophant by default; prompt it to challenge you, not validate you
- Write the work you need to write, not the work you think will sell — authenticity outlasts strategy
- Persistence after rejection is table stakes; the real edge is writing/building something that genuinely matters to you

### Relationships

- Your choice of partner is your highest-leverage life decision — a great spouse makes everything easier; a bad one makes everything harder
- Adult friendships decay by default without intentional scheduling — fight for them actively or lose them passively
- Over-surveilling your kids (constant location tracking, strict accountability) backfires — teach judgment, not just compliance
- When parenting athletes, back off — excessive pressure, private coaches, and school transfers for sports typically harm development and enjoyment

### Business

- In a 50/50 co-founder split, explicitly discuss and agree on who makes final calls — ambiguity creates paralysis and resentment
- Retention deserves as much attention as hiring — most businesses over-invest in recruiting and under-invest in keeping top talent
- Before raising prices, exhaust the five internal revenue levers: upsell, cross-sell, reactivate past clients, improve conversion, increase frequency
- Invest heavily in employee development and clear growth paths — top performers leave when they stop growing, not just when underpaid
- Build brand presence on LinkedIn now — AI search tools (ChatGPT, Claude) are citing LinkedIn content in answers; invisible brands lose

### AI & Technology

- Claude Cowork connects to your local files for context-aware AI assistance — useful for knowledge workers managing large document libraries
- For AI-generated content to rank in search or be cited by AI: prioritize E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals on all web pages
- ChatGPT advertising (sponsored placements in AI answers) is emerging — understand how to structure content to be recommended, not just found
- Use AI tools for execution (drafting, coding, research) but retain your own judgment for strategy and decisions

### Real Estate & Urban Development

- New housing supply — even 'luxury' — improves overall affordability by filtering up demand and freeing older stock
- Development charges are a primary driver of housing cost increases — policy changes here matter more than most other interventions
- In deal-making, 'no' is the second best answer — prolonged ambiguity wastes more time and resources than a fast rejection
- Canadian developers building in the US face significant banking and legal friction — plan for 12–18 months of setup complexity before first close

### Personal Finance

- File taxes promptly — submit all T1 checklist documents to your accountant early; processing backlogs hit late submitters hardest
- In closed-end real estate funds (5–15 year horizons), patience is the core discipline — return multiples compress dramatically when investors exit early under pressure
